OBSERVATIONS FOR PHASE 3:
  - C baseline is 1.27-3.03x faster than NumPy (Python overhead dominates at small V)
  - At large V (32k-128k), gap narrows — both are memory-bandwidth bound
  - Horizontal reduction is the known weakness: GCC emits scalar vaddss per element
    rather than a ymm shuffle+hadd sequence. This is where ASM can win.
  - Normalise pass: GCC emits scalar vdivss + vbroadcastss rather than vdivps ymm.
    Hand-written vdivps will process 8 elements per cycle vs 1.
